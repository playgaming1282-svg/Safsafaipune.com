Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before booking our team.

How long does a 2 BHK deep cleaning take? ▼
Usually a full 2 BHK deep cleaning takes between 4 to 6 hours depending on the furnishing and condition of the flat. A dedicated team of 2-3 cleaners is deployed.
Do I need to provide any machines, ladders or cleaning liquids? ▼
No! Our team comes equipped with commercial single-disc floor scrubbers, wet/dry vacuum cleaners, professional ladders, microfiber wipes, and Diversey Taski chemicals. All we need is electricity and continuous water supply.
Are your cleaning chemicals safe for kids and pets? ▼
Yes, we strictly avoid raw muriatic acids that ruin tile glaze and emit choking fumes. We use certified, eco-friendly tile descalers and sanitizers safe for pets and children.
When do I make the payment? ▼
You only pay after our cleaning supervisor walks you through the entire apartment and you are 100% satisfied with the finish. We accept UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm) and Cash.

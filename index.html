FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions before booking.

How long does a full home deep cleaning take? ▼
A standard 1 BHK or 2 BHK takes between 4 to 6 hours, while 3 BHK and 4 BHK flats take 6 to 8 hours depending on property size and condition.
Do I need to arrange buckets, ladders, or detergents? ▼
No. Our team comes fully prepared with single-disc floor machines, vacuum cleaners, ladders, microfiber wipers, and Taski cleaning chemicals. We only require running water and an electrical connection.
Can I book only single items like sofa, kitchen, or chairs? ▼
Yes! You can book individual items like chair wash (₹149), window track cleaning (₹199), kitchen (₹1,299), or sofa shampooing (₹799) without booking the full house.
When do I make the payment? ▼
No advance payment is needed. You only pay after the cleaning is finished and you have verified the work. Payment can be made via UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm) or cash.
